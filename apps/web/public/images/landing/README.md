# Landing update paper

`update-paper.webp` is the decorative background for `UpdateNote`. The note's
date and five changes are normal HTML, maintained in `landing-content.ts`.
The folded corner is CSS so it stays sharp at every screen size.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Three variants were compared:
plain ivory, graph paper and ruled paper. Plain ivory was selected because
its small blue doodles match the landing palette and its clear center keeps
the update copy readable. The selected image was resized to 1088 px wide and
encoded as WebP (quality 85).

Selected prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: subtle notebook paper background for a Polish student study app landing page. Generate a single portrait 3:4 sheet texture, completely filling the image edge to edge, perfectly flat top-down with no perspective or exterior background. Clean warm ivory paper (#f4f1e8), very fine natural paper fibers, minimalist editorial quality. Very small sparse hand-drawn blue graphite study doodles ONLY at extreme outer margins: one tiny tic-tac-toe grid near bottom right, one small mathematical summation near upper left edge, a tiny looping arrow near bottom left. All doodles together occupy under 4 percent of sheet. The entire center 85 percent must be blank clean space for real HTML text overlay. No main writing, no headings, no paragraphs, no logos, no watermark, no border, no fold, no shadow, no objects. Quiet refined modern feel to match black and electric blue typography.
